## The lab

We work in the [PACCAR Environmental Technology Building](https://cplinc.com/work/projects/paccar-environmental-technology-building/),
a LEED Gold facility shared across the environmental disciplines. The group runs
an 840 square foot biosafety level 2 environmental virology laboratory built for
molecular work on water samples, from concentration through sequencing.
Computation runs on the university's [Kamiak](https://hpc.wsu.edu/) cluster, where
the group holds a dedicated node.

{% include section.html %}

## Instruments

- Oxford Nanopore P2 Solo long-read sequencer
- Qiagen QIAcuity digital polymerase chain reaction
- Bio-Rad CFX96 quantitative polymerase chain reaction
- Agilent TapeStation 4150
- Biosafety level 2 and 2+ culture facilities

program, and positions for undergraduate researchers. You do not need a background
in microbiology; the methods are learned on the way.
