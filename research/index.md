# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-microscope" %}Research

Six threads, one question: what is living in this water, where did it come from,
and will the treatment in front of us actually remove it.

{% include section.html %}

## Threads

**Wastewater-based epidemiology.** Reading community infection from what arrives
at the treatment plant. We work on what a wastewater measurement can validly tell
you, and where that inference breaks.

**Virus removal and water reuse.** Membrane filtration, disinfection, and log
removal verification — including whether membranes can replace chlorine for virus
removal at a working reclamation facility.

**Prophage ecology.** Phages integrated in bacterial genomes, what induces them,
and what they carry with them when they move.

**Source tracking, fate and transport.** Attributing contamination to specific
sources, and modelling where it travels, using watershed simulation with global
sensitivity analysis.

**Biofilms and particle interactions.** How community structure forms on surfaces,
and how viruses associate with microplastics and other particles that shelter them
from treatment.

**Environmental bioinformatics.** Long-read and short-read sequencing, metagenome
assembly, and the open, reproducible pipelines that make the results checkable by
someone else.

{% include section.html %}

## Current projects

**Membrane filtration as a replacement for chlorine disinfection** for virus
removal at a regional water reclamation facility, with the City of Spokane and
Spokane County.

**Ultrafiltration for virus removal**, with the Water Research Foundation.
