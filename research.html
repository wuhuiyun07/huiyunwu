Research

What is living in this water, where did it come from, and will the treatment remove it?

Wastewater-based epidemiology

Reading community infection from what arrives at the treatment plant. We work on what a wastewater measurement can validly tell you, and where that inference breaks.

Virus removal and water reuse

Membrane filtration, disinfection, and log removal verification — including whether membranes can replace chlorine for virus removal at a working reclamation facility.

Prophage ecology

Phages integrated in bacterial genomes, what induces them, and what they carry with them when they move.

Source tracking, fate and transport

Attributing contamination to specific sources, and modelling where it travels, using watershed simulation with global sensitivity analysis.

Biofilms and particle interactions

How community structure forms on surfaces, and how viruses associate with microplastics and other particles that shelter them from treatment.

Environmental bioinformatics

Long-read and short-read sequencing, metagenome assembly, and the open, reproducible pipelines that make the results checkable by someone else.
