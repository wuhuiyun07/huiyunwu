Research

What is living in this water, where did it come from, and will the treatment remove it?

Wastewater-based epidemiology

Reading community infection from what arrives at the treatment plant, and where that inference breaks.

Virus removal and water reuse

Membrane filtration, disinfection, and log removal verification, including whether membranes can replace chlorine.

Prophage ecology

Phages integrated in bacterial genomes, what induces them, and what they carry when they move.

Source tracking and transport

Attributing contamination to sources and modelling where it travels, with watershed simulation and global sensitivity analysis.

Biofilms and particles

How communities form on surfaces, and how viruses associate with microplastics and other particles that shelter them from treatment.

Environmental bioinformatics

Long-read and short-read sequencing, metagenome assembly, and pipelines another person can check.

Current projects

Spokane. Membrane filtration as a replacement for chlorine disinfection at a regional water reclamation facility, with the City of Spokane and Spokane County.
Water Research Foundation. Ultrafiltration for virus removal.
