Who works here

Huiyun Wu, Ph.D.

Principal investigator · Washington State University

Assistant Professor of Civil and Environmental Engineering. Her work runs from field sampling at treatment plants through molecular and sequencing methods to the models and risk assessments that utilities and regulators use.

She joined Washington State University in 2024. Before that she held a postdoctoral appointment in Environmental Health Sciences at Tulane University with Tiong Gim Aw, and spent three years as a research fellow at the United States Environmental Protection Agency, Office of Research and Development, with Marirosa Molina, working on water reuse.

She completed her doctorate and master's degree in environmental engineering at Michigan State University with Irene Xagoraraki, and holds a bachelor's degree in environmental science. She passed the Principles and Practice of Engineering examination in environmental engineering in 2020.

She serves on the Groundwater Technical Advisory Committee of the Washington State Department of Ecology, and on a Water Research Foundation project advisory committee. She teaches stormwater management and hazardous contaminant pathway analysis.

The lab

We work in the PACCAR Environmental Technology Building, a LEED Gold facility shared across the environmental disciplines. The group runs an 840 square foot biosafety level 2 environmental virology laboratory built for molecular work on water samples, from concentration through sequencing. Computation runs on the university's Kamiak cluster, where the group holds a dedicated node.

Instruments

  • Oxford Nanopore P2 Solo long-read sequencer
  • Qiagen QIAcuity digital polymerase chain reaction
  • Bio-Rad CFX96 quantitative polymerase chain reaction
  • Agilent TapeStation 4150
  • Biosafety level 2 and 2+ culture facilities
